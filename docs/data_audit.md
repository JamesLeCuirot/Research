## Duplicates and label consistency

65 groups of byte-identical trajectories cover 166 rows. In 59 of these groups, the copies carry **different** MAST labels.

| Duplicate set | Pairs | Mean per-code Cohen's κ | Raw agreement | Identical label sets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| magentic_cross_group | 30 | 0.43 | 0.80 | 4 |
| chatdev_codellama | 111 | -0.04 | 0.71 | 4 |

κ is computed separately for each of the 14 codes and then averaged (codes with no variation are skipped). Raw agreement looks high because most codes are 'no' most of the time; κ corrects for that.

Per-code κ for the Magentic pairs: 1.1 0.59, 1.2 -0.05, 1.3 0.60, 1.4 0.35, 1.5 0.64, 2.1 0.46, 2.2 0.59, 2.3 0.23, 2.4 0.21, 2.5 0.53, 2.6 0.67, 3.1 0.57, 3.2 0.19, 3.3 0.47

The Magentic pairs are the same 30 traces filed under two group names; their κ is a usable test-retest figure for MAST's LLM judge (small sample: treat as indicative). The ChatDev-CodeLlama copies agree at about chance level, which suggests the text and labels in that group were mismatched upstream; the whole group is excluded.

## MAST's human-labelled file (`MAD_human_labelled_dataset.json`)

19 traces (the paper and some documentation say 21). Each round used a different revision of the taxonomy, so codes are not comparable across rounds.

| Round | Traces | Modes in taxonomy | Distinct 3-annotator label vectors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | 5 | 18 | 2 |
| Round 2 | 5 | 17 | 2 |
| Round 3 | 5 | 17 | 2 |
| Generlazability | 4 | 14 | 2 |

Within every round, different traces carry identical label vectors. The original annotation sheets in MAST's GitHub repo (`inter_annotator_agreement_annotations/*.pdf`) show different labels per trace, so the Hugging Face file appears to have been assembled incorrectly. We do not use it.
