# Independent audit: "From RSP to SMS" (paper.md, draft v0.1)

Auditor: independent fact-check (Claude, AI-assisted), 3 November 2026. `paper.md` was not edited. Every source below was re-opened in this session; `sources.md` was used only to locate sources. Working copies are in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.

## Summary

About 160 specific factual claims, quotations, numbers and locators were checked. Each of the 33 references was re-opened, and all 45 cells of Table 2 were read against the source texts.

| Category | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| VERIFIED | about 136 | Includes all direct quotations from the RSP, the Risk Report, the four incident posts, the metrics page, the OpenAI and GDM frameworks, the EU Code, Annex 19, Amendment 2, Doc 9859, 14 CFR Part 5 and EASA/UK ORO text. Every quotation checked was verbatim (apart from ellipses). |
| WRONG | 8 | Two are substantive (W1, W2); one is substantive for OpenAI and the EU Code (W3); the rest are minor. |
| OVERSTATED | 15 | Most concern claims of absence ("not described", "no framework") that are contradicted by text in the cited sources or in public companion documents. |
| UNSUPPORTED | 1 | An occurrence-taxonomy claim made without examining the public compliance frameworks. |
| NOT ACCESSIBLE | 4 | OpenAI website (HTTP 403), Anthropic Frontier Compliance Framework PDF, ICAO originals (HTTP 403), and the original ICAO text of Annex 6 Part I. |
| OMISSION (bears on conclusions) | 5 | The most important is that public companion documents (Anthropic's 31 August 2026 post, Anthropic's FCF, OpenAI's FGF, California SB 53, AI Act Art. 55) were not considered. |
| Reference / provenance | 8 reference entries with issues; 25 with none | The main problem is that Doc 9859 is cited from a non-authoritative third-party copy. |

**Overall judgement.** The aviation side is accurate. Annex 19 element names and numbering, the Amendment 2 redline, the Doc 9859 paragraph numbers, 14 CFR Part 5 section numbers and dates, and ORO.GEN.200/ORO.AOC.130 all checked out. The descriptive passages on Anthropic's artefacts are also accurate. The weak point is the paper's claims of absence. Several of them are contradicted by text in the cited sources: OpenAI PF v2 App. C.3 (change management, independent security audits, 24x7 incident response), the Anthropic Roadmap (detection metrics with defined targets), and the EU Code (near-miss triggers, Measure 7.6 change trigger, App. 4.4 training). Others are contradicted by public documents the paper did not consider: Anthropic's 31 August 2026 post, Anthropic's SB 53 Frontier Compliance Framework, and OpenAI's Frontier Governance Framework. The headline conclusions survive in softened form. Section 5.2 ("in the public documents, the main change that triggers assessment is a new or more capable model"), Section 5.4, Section 5.5 and several Table 2 comments must be rewritten.
