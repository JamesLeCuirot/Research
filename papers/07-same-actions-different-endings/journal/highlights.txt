Highlights

- On average, AI models judged the same crew actions more culpable after a bad ending
- Within-case design: 48 real ASRS occurrences, each with a safe and a bad ending
- Shift came mainly via the substitution test; for DeepSeek, also via knowing violation
- Clear blameless and reckless control cases were unaffected by the ending
- Same text, same model: verdicts differed across five repeats in 18-28% of cells
