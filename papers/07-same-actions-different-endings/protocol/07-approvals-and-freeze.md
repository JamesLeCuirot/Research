**The pilot result** (1 Oct 2026, after D8 and D9): Claude Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol and DeepSeek V4 Pro each returned 40 of 40 valid pilot replies, with every field parsed. The pilot spent $2.50 in total, including $1.28 on the dropped Gemini model. The cost per valid reply was $0.0203 for Claude, $0.0075 for GPT and $0.0021 for DeepSeek. The projected cost of the main run (1,080 calls per model, controls included) is about $32, plus retries.
