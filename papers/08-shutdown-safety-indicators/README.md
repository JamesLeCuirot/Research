# 08 — Safety indicators at US airports during the October–November 2025 government shutdown

**Status (11 November 2026):** pre-registered; data processing not yet started. No results exist.

A pre-registered study using the adsb.lol open ADS-B archive. It compares go-around rates, final-approach spacing and TCAS resolution advisories at 55 US airports during the shutdown with the same weekday-aligned dates in 2024 and with 29 Canadian and European airports.

- [`PREREGISTRATION.md`](PREREGISTRATION.md): hypotheses, windows, measures, exclusions, analysis and detectable effect, committed before any study-window data were processed.
- [`code/`](code/): extraction (`extract.py`), approach and go-around detection (`approaches.py`), the fixed airport and aircraft-type lists, and the resumable runners.
