# 08 · How often do crews land with the wrong altimeter setting, and who does?

**Status:** pre-registered 11 November 2026; data analysis not yet run.

A pre-registered study using open ADS-B data (adsb.lol) to measure how often aircraft land with the crew-selected altimeter setting differing from the local setting, at 119 US and European airports on 30 days in 2026, and to compare business jets with airline jets and the US with Europe.

- [`PREREGISTRATION.md`](PREREGISTRATION.md): hypotheses, measures and analysis, committed before any altimeter-setting data from the study days was read.
- [`code/`](code/): frozen code, with SHA-256 hashes in `code/HASHES.txt`.

AI assistance: design, code and analysis by Claude (Anthropic) under the author's direction.
