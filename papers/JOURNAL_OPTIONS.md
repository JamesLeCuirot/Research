# Journal options for papers 01, 02, 06 and 07

Checked on 4 November 2026 on each journal's or publisher's own pages where they could be opened. Several publishers block automated access: Elsevier's guides, Taylor & Francis journal pages, Wiley, ACM and Sherpa Romeo. Points taken from search results or third-party pages are marked *(secondary)*. Elsevier's fees come from its own price list (dated 5 October 2026). *The Aeronautical Journal* was confirmed on Cambridge's pages and spot-checked again.

**Why the AI policy column matters:** all four papers were drafted with AI help under the author's direction, and say so. A journal's policy on AI-assisted writing is the first filter:
- **Allowed if declared:** Elsevier, Springer, Cambridge, Taylor & Francis (if "rigorously revised"), TMLR, IJAAA.
- **Ruled out:**
  - *AI & Society* ("strongly discouraged");
  - *Human Factors* (copy-editing only);
  - *JAAER* (prohibits "Drafting the manuscript").

## Shortlist per paper

### 01 Just Culture for Machines (theory, ~7,400 words)

1. ***Safety Science*** (Elsevier). Readers know just culture. Free unless you choose open access ($4,150). Package ready; strengthen the safety-science grounding first (reading pack in `01-…/READING_PACK.md`).
2. ***Cognition, Technology & Work*** (Springer). Safety and accidents in sociotechnical systems, aviation named in its scope; takes theory papers. LLM use allowed if documented. Impact factor 2.8; median 8 days to first decision; free route.
3. ***Philosophy & Technology*** (Springer). Suits the conceptual argument and the "where the analogy fails" section; double-anonymised. Alternative: *AI and Ethics* (Springer), with a wider AI-ethics readership.
4. ***The Aeronautical Journal*** (RAeS/Cambridge), only if reframed for aviation readers (what ASRS, ASAP and Regulation 376/2014 teach about AI used in aviation). It is engineering-led, so there is some risk of rejection as out of scope.
   - *Wildcard:* *The Alignment Journal*. New and unindexed, allows "full use of LLMs", no fees.

### 02 Do LLM Monitors Miss Rare Threats? (pre-registered experiment)

1. **TMLR.** Package ready. No fees, no length limit; LLMs allowed "as general-purpose assistive tools". A sole author gets two submissions a year.
2. ***Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research*** (JAIR). Open access, no fees, no length limit, 8–12 weeks to decision. Its policy says "the core contributions of a paper must come from the authors"; your design and pre-registration support that.
3. ***Applied Ergonomics*** (Elsevier). The human-factors route; it publishes airport X-ray screening and automation research, so the low-prevalence framing fits. Free route ($3,400 for open access). Alternative: *Ergonomics* (Taylor & Francis).
4. ***The Alignment Journal.*** Scalable oversight is in scope.

### 06 AFACS (framework, dataset audit, AI-coded pilot)

1. **TMLR.** Package ready, but it uses your second TMLR submission of the year.
2. ***Journal of Data-centric Machine Learning Research*** (DMLR). The audit of the MAST dataset is squarely "data quality". Single-blind (no anonymising), no fees, no page limit. Using it keeps the TMLR quota free for paper 02.
3. ***Cognition, Technology & Work***, after the human-coder study: human-factors reviewers will expect human coders.
4. ***Safety Science*** or ***Theoretical Issues in Ergonomics Science***, after the human-coder study.

### 07 Outcome Bias in AI Models (pre-registered experiment, ~5,900 words)

1. ***Safety Science.*** Package ready. It published Oğuz et al. (2026), the closest related work. Free route. Double-anonymised, so anonymity is only partial.
2. ***The Aeronautical Journal*** (RAeS/Cambridge). Reaches operators and investigators.
   - **Single-anonymised review,** so reviewers see your name and the anonymity problem goes away.
   - Preprints allowed; AI use must be declared.
   - Under 10,000 words, APA references, a nomenclature section required.
   - 3–6 months review; free unless open access (£2,610).
3. ***International Journal of Aerospace Psychology*** (Taylor & Francis). Core aviation-psychology readership; double-anonymised; AI text allowed if rigorously revised and disclosed. Limits not checked (site blocked).
4. ***Journal of Safety Research*** (Elsevier; double-anonymised; free route). Or ***Judgment and Decision Making*** (Cambridge): outcome bias is a core topic and pre-registration is valued, but it is fully open access at £2,610 unless waived.
   - *Fast free fallback:* ***IJAAA*** (Embry-Riddle). No fees; its policy names Claude as allowed if disclosed; decisions within 90 days. Limit about 25 double-spaced pages including references; low visibility.

## All journals checked

Policy groups:
- **Elsevier:** drafting support allowed, but not "Generating sections of a manuscript without genuine intellectual contribution from the author"; a declaration is required.
- **Springer:** "Use of an LLM should be properly documented in the Methods section"; "there must be human accountability for the final version of the text".
- **Taylor & Francis:** not permitted is "Text or code generation without rigorous revision"; disclosure required.
- **Cambridge:** declare AI use that generates text or images or analyses data.
