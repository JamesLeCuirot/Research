How to review a case (2 minutes to read)

You are reviewing an investigation report someone else wrote, not coding from scratch. The question each time is whether the AI's account is supported by the evidence.

  1. Read The question for you and How this system works on the right.
  2. Read the AI's summary, then click its step numbers. The trace on the left jumps to each cited step, with the quoted words highlighted.
  3. Skim the rest of the trace. You don't need to follow the code; the agents' own words and the error messages carry the story.
  4. Answer the review questions. "Can't judge" is a fine answer. The free-text boxes matter most: say what you'd write differently as an investigator.

If a step is unclear, ask Claude in chat, e.g. "what does step S5 in K2 mean?". You'll get an explanation of the mechanics, not a verdict. Answers save automatically to this page's private store; only you can write them.
